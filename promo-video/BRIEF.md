# Chronos promo

30 seconds · 1920 × 1080 · 30 fps · English

Audience: company administrators and employees.
Style: bold typography, teal/indigo gradients, fast animated illustrative workflow cards.
Audio: original 144 BPM broadcast-style cue with minor-key bass pulses, kick/snare,
hi-hats, brass-like accents, strings and transition risers. Locally synthesized;
no voiceover or external samples. Generated by `make_newsroom.py`.
The cards use fictional demonstration data, not screenshots of the live application.

| Time | Message | Visual |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3.33s | Make time for what matters. | Animated clock and Chronos wordmark |
| 3.33–6.67s | Every hour. In view. | Weekly time-entry card |
| 6.67–10s | Projects. In focus. | Logged and planned project hours |
| 10–13.33s | Expenses. Accounted for. | Expense claim and receipt attachment |
| 13.33–16.67s | Time off. Less back-and-forth. | Leave request moving to approved |
| 16.67–20s | Keep work moving. | Timesheet and leave approval cards |
| 20–23.33s | Your people. Connected. | Team roles and company announcements |
| 23.33–26.67s | Ready for the next step. | Approved timesheet export |
| 26.67–30s | Your team. Your time. Meet Chronos. | Brand closing frame |

Eight 10-frame directional slides and wipes overlap adjacent scenes. New scenes
start every 100 frames (eight beats at 144 BPM); total remains 900 frames.

Claims are based on the repository README and implemented timesheet, leave,
project, expense, employee dashboard, and reporting UI.
No pricing, availability, compliance certification, or performance claims are included.

## Preview

From `promo-video`, run `npm run dev`, then select **ChronosPromo**.
Each scene also has its own composition for editing.

## Export

`npx remotion render src/index.ts ChronosPromo out/chronos-promo.mp4`

The preview is the initial deliverable; MP4 export can be requested separately.
